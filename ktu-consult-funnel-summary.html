Cancellations worsening

54% in June → 65% in July → 73% in August. August is the worst month in the twelve except November 2025, and the trend has run one direction since May.

Attended consults halved

29 in May → 18 June → 17 July → 12 in August. Bookings held flat at 44–49 the whole time. The funnel isn't losing leads, it's losing visits.

Proposal acceptance collapsed

Steady 42–65% for ten months, then 28% in July and 35% in August. Both are the worst readings in the year.

Proposals per visit spiking

July issued 2.12 proposals per attended consult against a 1.5 average. More options written per customer, far fewer signed — quoting volume went up as close rate went down.

Cancel-reason codes appear on 11 of 39 (id 3523 = "Other", id 4279 = "Duplicate Booking").

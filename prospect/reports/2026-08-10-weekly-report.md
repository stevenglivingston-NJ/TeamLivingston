## 1. Executive Summary

This first scan reviewed cross-market sources (LoopNet, Crexi, Real Estate NJ, ROI-NJ, Jersey Digs, Village Green NJ, Montclair Local, New York YIMBY, Kislak/Gebroe-Hammer/Berkadia broker press) plus targeted searches across every primary-corridor municipality and the secondary volume market. **14 leads were sourced and entered into the master list** — well short of the 50-lead aspiration in the agent brief. Section 7 explains why in detail; the short version is that several of the registry's stated "stable" sources (re-nj.com, montclairlocal.news, newyorkyimby.com) returned bot-verification walls or HTTP 403 on direct fetch this week, so those stories were captured only via search-result snippets, and several real transactions this week had **undisclosed buyers**, which caps how far a lead can be enriched under the agent's no-invented-facts rule.

**Leads reviewed:** ~30 candidate signals surfaced across searches; **14 met the bar for a sourced, dated master-list entry**; **5 qualify for the immediate-action table** below (scores 45–80); **1 lead scored in the 75+ "outreach this week" band**.

**Top municipalities this week:** Montclair (6 of 14 leads — the single most active submarket, consistent with the target-market map's read of it as the corridor's largest premium rental market), Livingston (2 leads, both large planned/under-construction projects), East Orange/Orange/Newark/Nutley (secondary market, 6 leads/relationship items).

**Three most attractive opportunities:**

1. **59-65 Church St, Montclair (Dobco Group / Daniel Mladenovic)** — score 80. A 74-unit shell that stalled for years under a prior developer is being taken over by Dobco Group following a $24.6M foreclosure sale, with the township about to formalize the redevelopment-agreement and PILOT transfer (vote scheduled 2026-09-15). The interior/kitchen package is very likely still open. This is the clearest "get in before spec-lock" opportunity found this week.
2. **47 Union St, Montclair** — score 55. A 25-unit prewar building sold in January 2026 with only 15 of 25 units renovated; the remaining ~10 units are a plausible near-term unit-turn kitchen scope, though the buyer's identity is not yet public, so this is a broker-referral play (via Berkadia) rather than a direct-contact one.
3. **Livana Livingston (Kushner Companies)** — score 55. A 280-unit, two-building project broke ground in Livingston in mid-2025 with a 2027 completion target — early enough that FF&E/kitchen procurement is plausibly still open, in the corridor's top-school-district submarket.

**Market pattern observed:** the primary corridor's live activity this week skews toward **new/resuming construction** (Church St, Lackawanna Plaza, Livana Livingston, the Livingston Mall Sears site) rather than **acquisitions of dated, occupied buildings** — the classic Offer B/C trigger. Several buildings that did trade this week (47 Union St, Franklin Towers in Bloomfield, the Kislak East Orange/Orange portfolio) were reported as *already* substantially renovated or mid-renovation, meaning the kitchen-CapEx decision for those specific units has already been made by someone else. That suggests this week's best fit is Offer D (boutique/large new-development kitchen packages) more than Offers B/C, and that acquisition-stage Offer A/B opportunities may be under-represented in this scan because active listings' full marketing descriptions (condition, unit mix, photos) were not accessible through the search tools used this week — a gap flagged for next week (see §7).

### Rank 1 — 59-65 Church St, Montclair (Dobco Group / Daniel Mladenovic)

**Thesis:** Dobco Group is about to formalize legal and financial control (redevelopment-agreement transfer, PILOT) over a 74-unit shell that stalled for years under a prior developer. That means the interior finish package — including kitchens — has almost certainly not been re-awarded since Dobco took over. This is the single best-timed opportunity in this week's scan to get in ahead of spec-lock in the corridor's largest premium submarket.

**Relevance proof:** $24.6M foreclosure purchase (Sept 2025) of a stalled 74-unit, 8-affordable-unit, ~3,936 sf-retail building; Montclair Township is scheduled to vote 2026-09-15 on transferring the redevelopment agreement and tax abatement to the new owner — both facts corroborated across independent search-result citations of Montclair Local's reporting.

**Pitch:** Standardized, per-unit-priced kitchen packages with coordinated procurement and phased installation — built for exactly this situation: a builder restarting a stalled project on a compressed schedule who needs predictable finish-out costs to firm up their underwriting.

**Email (subject: "Kitchen CapEx range for 59-65 Church St"):**
> Daniel — saw that the redevelopment agreement transfer for 59-65 Church is moving through Council this month, which usually means interior finish decisions are close behind. We put together standardized kitchen packages (design, per-unit pricing, coordinated procurement, phased install) specifically for multifamily projects restarting after a stall, where predictable CapEx numbers matter for underwriting. Worth a 15-minute call to walk through a CapEx range for the 66 market-rate units?

**LinkedIn note (≤300 chars):**
> Daniel — following the Church St redevelopment-agreement transfer through Montclair Council. We build standardized kitchen packages for multifamily restarts like this — predictable per-unit CapEx, phased install. Open to a quick call once the Sept 15 vote closes?

**Phone opener:** "Hi Daniel, this is [name] — I saw the Church Street redevelopment agreement is up for a Council vote this month. I run a kitchen-repositioning shop that does standardized packages for exactly this kind of restart project. Do you have two minutes, or should I follow up after the vote?"

**Likely objection + response:** *"We already have our finish package sourced."* → "Totally understand — happy to leave a CapEx benchmark sheet for the file in case a second bid is useful for underwriting comparison, no pressure either way."

**Verify before outreach:**
- Confirm the 2026-09-15 council vote actually passed and the redevelopment agreement/PILOT transfer is final.

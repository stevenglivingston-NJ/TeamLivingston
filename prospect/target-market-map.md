## 2. Property priority tiers

- **Tier 1 (immediate):** 4–40-unit buildings in premium submarkets; generally
  pre-1990 with plausibly dated kitchens; prewar walk-ups, converted homes,
  garden apartments, mixed-use residential; near rail/village centers; with a
  live capital event (listed, under contract, recently sold/refinanced).
- **Tier 2 (scalable):** owners/operators with 2–10+ local buildings; 25–150
  unit portfolios or management assignments; rolling vacancy and below-market
  rents → Unit-Turn Program candidates.
- **Tier 3 (selective projects):** boutique 20–80-unit new construction,
  adaptive reuse, condo conversion, mixed-use, luxury rental/townhomes — in
  planning through preconstruction, before finish specs lock.
- **Tier 4 (relationship-only):** 100+ unit projects. Never pitch displacement
  of an awarded kitchen supplier; pursue model units, VE, premium upgrade
  units, punch-list, replacement doors/panels/hardware, appliance coordination,
  leasing-office/amenity millwork, turnover scopes, overflow subcontracting,
  and future preconstruction relationships.

### Deprioritize / exclude

Single-family (unless affluent-buyer renovation intent + referral source);
price-only distressed housing; buildings with no credible renovation or
ownership-change signal; fully stabilized projects without a turnover/CapEx
trigger; leads with no identifiable decision-maker path unless the property
score is extremely high.

## 3. Signals to monitor (weekly)

1. **Transaction:** new listings (7/14/30-day windows); "value-add", "upside",
   "below-market rents", "renovation potential", "estate sale", "as-is",
   "investor", "vacant units", "conversion", "walk to train", "luxury rental",
   "designer" language; dated kitchens in listing photos; pending/under
   contract where public; deed transfers; mortgages/construction loans/refis/
   assignments of rents; LLC ownership changes; broker announcements.
2. **Renovation/permit:** building permits where public; planning & zoning
   agendas/minutes/packets; redevelopment plans; historic-preservation and
   site-plan applications; major interior alteration/conversion/change-of-use;
   multifamily/mixed-use/adaptive-reuse/condo/townhome applications; local
   construction news.
3. **Owner/portfolio:** multi-property owners; LLCs linked to multiple
   buildings; common management; PMs operating in the corridor; repeat local
   developers/owner-operators/family offices; growth hires/postings in
   construction, asset management, leasing, PM.
4. **High-rise relationships:** approved/planned 75+ unit projects in the
   secondary market and suburban nodes; named developer/GC/architect/owner rep/
   interior designer/millwork sub/leasing agent; stage; any public signal that
   kitchen/cabinet/millwork/appliance procurement is not yet awarded.

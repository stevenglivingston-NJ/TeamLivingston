## 1. The headline finding

The refacing campaign (`009-S-Cabinet Refacing`) spent **≈$2,645 over 90 days for 4 conversions (~$661/lead)**, and every one of those conversions came from a single broad-match keyword (`reface cabinets near me`, $408/lead). Every phrase/exact refacing keyword converted at **zero**.

The root cause is measurable, not a guess: **Quality Score 2/10 on `kitchen cabinet refacing near me`** (the highest-volume high-intent term in the market, 5,400 searches/mo) with **landing page experience rated BELOW AVERAGE** by Google. The market CPC for that term is **$7.68** (Semrush); the account is paying **$22–$66+ effective CPC** on refacing clicks — a 3–8× Quality Score penalty. Fixing the landing page is the single highest-leverage paid-ads move available.

## 5. Landing page audit — `ktubloomfield.com/kitchen-cabinet-refacing`

Google itself rates this page "below average" on the keywords that matter. What's right and wrong:

**Working:** dedicated refacing page (not the homepage), honest price range ($15k–$45k+), "one week or less" timeline, 0% financing / no deposit, no-subcontractors + background-checked staff, Essex County town list, before/after photos.

**Broken / missing (in priority order):**
1. **"Our Easy 4 Step Process" is a heading with no steps** — a literally broken section on a paid page.
2. **No review count or star rating anywhere** — one testimonial (Sharon N). Home Depot leads with "Guaranteed"; Premier shows "4.9 stars, 189+ reviews." KTU Bloomfield has GMB reviews that never appear here.
3. **Form is mid-page, not in the hero** — first CTA is a phone number; mobile paid traffic gets no immediate form.
4. **Two different phone numbers on one page** ((973) 521-1182 and (973) 521-8442) — splits call tracking and erodes trust.
5. **No message match for refinishing/painting/resurfacing searchers** — `cabinet refinishing near me` (12.1k/mo, QS 3) lands on a refacing-only page. KTU sells cabinet painting and redooring; the page never says so.
6. **No rebuttal to the "too expensive — just paint" attack** Premier runs in the same auctions. A reface vs. paint vs. replace comparison converts that objection.
7. **No FAQ** — cost/eligibility questions ("can laminate cabinets be refaced," "how much does refacing cost") are cheap, high-volume queries with no on-page answers.
8. **No explicit guarantee/warranty language.** Franchise backing makes this easy to add.
9. Before/afters have **no captions** (no town, timeline, or scope), muting their proof value.

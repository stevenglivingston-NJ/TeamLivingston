## Verify on site

**Measure**

- Room length × width, ceiling height
- Shower alcove width, depth, curb height
- Wet-wall square footage to the ceiling — the margin-critical number
- Floor square footage outside the shower
- Existing vanity width, and the widest cabinet the wall will take
- Window: distance from the shower, and whether any part sits inside the wet zone
- Radiator footprint and clearance to the vanity
- Door swing against a wider vanity

**Diagnose**

- **Why the shower failed** — cartridge, valve body, pan, or a leak. Determines whether Craig is in the job at all.
- Soft spots or staining on the floor outside the shower, and the ceiling below
- Substrate behind existing tile — greenboard, mud bed, or cement board
- Existing exhaust fan, and whether it actually vents outside
- Plaster or drywall — plaster changes the demo and patch story
- Shut-off valve locations and condition
- Photograph everything into CompanyCam; no project exists at this address yet

**If you find water damage.** A shower failing for two months in a century-old Glen Ridge house is the likeliest surprise. Set the subfloor/framing contingency expectation tomorrow rather than after signing.

Quick answers

The questions that come up most. Each links to the full rule.

Who places orders, and from what?

The PM (Mayra) for both brands, from the signed Selection Sheet only.

Purchasing orders from the client-signed Selection Sheet and the order sheet the pricing tool builds from it. Never from a rendering, a text or an unsigned draft. Order sheets are emailed to the recipients set in the pricing tool (default TLivingston@kitchentuneup.com). The order sheet →

Where do I log an order, ETA or actual cost?

The job's tab in the Job Tracker Google Sheet.

Drive → .Project Management → "Job Tracker — KTU & BTU (live)". Type in the Order status, PO, dates, Actual cost and Notes columns; the rest fills itself from JobTread. The Job Tracker →

What goes in the handover email?

Signed Design Packet + signed Selection Sheet, scope summary and watch-outs, in one email.

BTU: use the template in BTU-OPS-001 §7.2. KTU: the ten requirements in the signed standard. A message saying "it's on JobTread" is not a handover. BTU template → · KTU requirements →

The client wants to change something after signing.

Written, priced, signed change order first, then a new drawing revision.

Nothing is ordered or built differently until the change order is signed and the affected documents are re-issued as R+1. Change orders →

Which system is the truth for what?

Lead source: HighLevel. Appointments and money: ServiceMinder. Price and selections: JobTread. Photos: CompanyCam.

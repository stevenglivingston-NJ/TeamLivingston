Selections & Order Sheet

How selections are recorded so the client can sign them, purchasing can order from them without a phone call, and the job's real margin is known. One line standard for both brands.

Version 2.6 · Oct 2, 2026 Replaces Selections Checklist (Sept 23, 2026) Owners Sales (the sheet) · PM (ordering)
The ruleIf it isn't on the signed Selection Sheet, it doesn't get ordered. Every line is orderable as written: make, model/SKU, finish, quantity with unit, and one decided source. A "TBD" on a signed sheet is an unpriced change order waiting to happen.
After the saleOnce a job is sold (proposal accepted in ServiceMinder, the Sold gate closed, or JobTread Sales Status Sold or later), the JobTread budget belongs to ordering and production. pricing.ktubtu.com no longer rewrites it or the signed proposal. A price or scope change after the sale is a change order in JobTread, never a re-push from the pricing app.

The brand standards say the same thing in their own words: BTU-OPS-001 §5 and KTU Handover Standard item 10 ("the selection sheet is a deliverable, not a tracking document").

The selection appointment

The sections below are about the records. This one is about the meeting that produces them — where the job stops being an idea and becomes an order. Everything after it (the vendor order, the delivery date, the install window) is downstream of what gets decided here.

  • Book it before you leave the close. For a KTU reface the Handover Standard puts the selection appointment within 5 business days of contract, with the order placed within 7. For a custom kitchen the signed Design Brief comes first, within 5 business days, before any drafting hours are spent.
  • Present from the lookbook — lookbook.ktubtu.com, on the device, in the room. The tier ladder is the sales tool; a tool that isn’t opened in front of the client isn’t a tool.
  • The tier shown is the tier sold. Showing Signature and writing Essential on the sheet produces a disappointed client on install day and a business absorbing the difference.
  • Upgrades are a delta over the included allowance, and the delta must match what the pricing tool actually charges — not a remembered number. A wrong delta is margin lost silently; it only surfaces at job costing.
  • Only offer what exists. The bath books’ lighting and toilet pages build from the live JobTread Showroom overnight, so a discontinued product can still be on screen. If a client picks something unorderable, that is a feed problem, not a client problem — flag it the same day.
  • Finish it in the single visit. Track A targets 100% of the sheet in one appointment. If a second visit was needed, write down what forced it: that reason is the process defect, and it is the thing worth fixing.
  • Generate the packet from the tooling, never by hand. If a packet needed hand-editing to be presentable, record what you had to fix.
Why this sits hereThe line standard in §2 tells you what a finished line looks like. This section tells you how to run the hour that produces those lines. Both brands’ gates ultimately check the same thing: a sheet with no blanks, signed and dated, three weeks before job start.

1. Three records, three jobs

Client picks

Where: JobTread job → Selections tab (simple selections), or the pricing tool's client selection link.

Where the client chooses. One decision per sheet ("Choose your faucet"), every final pick ticked. It is a preference, not a signature, and never the order record. On the pricing tool's link the client can also leave a note on any line, send a product link for the designer to research, and approve — approval starts the final design; it is not a signature.

Selection Sheet (signed)

Where: JobTread job → Documents → New → Selections → template "Order Sheet — final selections (PDF)".

The signed, printable buy list. Built once the picks are final; prices hidden and excluded from the budget total (the proposal carries pricing). Signed the same day as the Design Packet.

Order sheet

Where: orders.ktubtu.com (pricing tool → job → Order sheet), and every job on one page at orders.ktubtu.com/w. The old Google Sheet "Job Tracker" is retired (§7).

Built from JobTread's finalized estimate plus the approved selections. Where the PM orders from, tracks each order, and records actual costs.

2. Every line on the Selection Sheet

  • Group = the category (Faucet, Lighting, Floor tile, Grout, Trim…), one group per category.
  • Name = make + model/collection + finish + SKU. Never just "Mirror".
  • Item & area for anything used in more than one place: "Wall tile – shower walls & tub surround".
  • Description = model/SKU, key sizes or specs, then Buy: [store] — [link]. One store, decided.
  • Fit notes where they matter: faucet holes, drain side, rough-in, opening size, valve body matched to trim.
  • Quantity + unit (each, box, sq ft, linear ft, gallon). Tile includes overage: 10% standard, 15% for diagonal, herringbone, large-format or patterned.
  • Supplied by BTU/KTU or the client (client-supplied items carry a delivery-by date; the client owns their warranty).
  • Status New · Existing – KEEP · Existing – REUSE/REINSTALL.
  • Cost code = the specific one (Plumbing: Bath Fixture, Electrical: Bathroom Fixture, Paint…), cost type Selection.
  • Photo attached. A screenshot is fine, but it doesn't replace the SKU.
  • Clean link: cut everything after ? unless it picks the finish (e.g. piid=, uid=).

3. The categories people forget

Every category is marked selected or N/A. None are left blank. The bathroom list is BTU-OPS-001 §5.2; these are the ones missed most often, on both brands:

  • Install materials: grout (type and color), Schluter/edge trim (profile and finish), floor transitions, waterproofing, backer board, wet-wall adhesive, thin-set, caulk color-matched to grout, sealer.
  • Plumbing guts: the rough-in valve body that matches the trim, shut-off valves/stops, supply lines, drains.
  • Shower details: niche size and ledge, bench or seat, glass (new or reused), drain style and finish.
  • Paint: brand, color name and code, sheen for walls, ceiling and trim.
  • Kitchen accessories: rollouts, lazy Susans, organizers and panel-ready appliance pulls, with quantities in the proposal (KTU item 4).
  • Lighting: every fixture on the drawing, including ceiling fixtures being replaced.
Why this list existsOn Sept 28 the PM had to send a sold job back for a niche ledge, grout colors, Schluter finish, a ceiling light and the countertop material. Every one of those belongs on the sheet before signature.

5. Before the sheet goes for signature

  1. Nothing is open. No "TBD", "not selected", "client to decide" or "see if X has it".
  2. Anything over allowance goes out as a change order first.
  3. The JobTread estimate lines match the sheet (same mirror, same sconce, same quantities) — the order sheet is built from the estimate, so a mismatch shows up as a wrong order.
  4. The Design Packet is at the same revision and will be signed the same day.
  5. Signature is on (the template does this).

After the client signs: download both PDFs, save them to the job's Drive folder with the standard file names, upload them to JobTread, and send the Handover Email (template). Purchasing orders from the signed sheet only.

6. The order sheet (pricing tool)

Status — live (Sept 29, 2026)The order sheet is live in the pricing tool. Open the job → Order sheet, or use the link in the order email.

When a client approves selections in the pricing tool, it builds the job's order sheet from JobTread's finalized estimate — quantities, costs and prices as quoted — and pairs each estimate line with the product the client chose for it.

Signature before any order (Sept 30, 2026)Until the final Selection Sheet is signed in JobTread, the order sheet reads Awaiting signed sheet and is a pre-order review only: check lead times and missing info, place nothing. The pricing tool refuses Ready for order and refuses marking a line Ordered, Shipped or Received until the signature is there. The approval email says "Pre-order review — DO NOT ORDER"; the signature sends "Signed — ready to order".

The client's selection link, stage by stage

  1. Sent — the designer shares the curated sheet (one link per job).
  2. In progress — the client and designer work it together. Every change is logged: old value → new value, who (client or team), when.
  3. Approved — the client approves. Blocked while a decision is open or a research link is unanswered. The orderers get a pre-order review; the designer gets the approval email.
  4. Final design & rendering — the design request goes out (re-sent automatically if one was sent before).
  5. Final design presented — the designer marks it. The client has one round of changes after approving; anything after that round, or after signing, is recorded as a change order requested and tracked to written or declined.
  6. Signed — read from the signed "Order Sheet — final selections (PDF)" in JobTread, never set by hand. Ordering opens once the PM passes the Production Gate.

Every client change and every stage move is emailed to the designer and the pricing tool's "selections" recipients, batched: one email per burst of edits. The tool's working-copy PDF is stamped "not signed, not an order" — the only signed sheet is the JobTread one.

  • One row per item: category, item, chosen product (make/model/finish), SKU, source, link, quantity and unit, expected unit cost, expected cost, expected retail, expected margin.
  • Grouped by vendor, so each group is one purchase order.
  • Needs info on any line missing SKU, finish, quantity or source. The sheet only reads Ready to order when there are none — the tool enforces the same rule as the Production Gate.
  • Labor, fees and services (delivery, sink cutout, project fee) appear for margin but are marked "not ordered". Install materials on the estimate (grout, setting materials, paint) show as Needs info until the sheet names them; general supplies show "Supplies — confirm"; materials a sub supplies (heated-floor mat) show "Sub supplies — confirm spec".
  • When the final Selection Sheet is signed, it sends the "Signed — ready to order" notice to the recipients set in the pricing tool's admin screen (default TLivingston@kitchentuneup.com, both brands) and drafts the Handover Email for Sales. The PM (Mayra) places orders for both brands.
  • The approved selections are written back to the JobTread job as a selections document. The job also gets a comment with the order sheet link whenever the sheet's status changes (not on every refresh).
  • Anyone on the team can rebuild it: open the job in the pricing tool → Order sheet. It also refreshes itself from JobTread every 2 hours, so a quantity fixed in JobTread reaches the order sheet.
Known data gapJobTread catalog items have no vendor or SKU field. The tool reads SKUs from item names and sources from links in the description, and anything it can't find is Needs info. The permanent fix is filling in make, model/SKU and a "Buy:" link on the catalog items themselves.

7. The Job Tracker (Google Sheet) — retired

Retired Oct 1, 2026The Google Sheet "Job Tracker — KTU & BTU (live)" no longer updates. It is frozen as it stood on Oct 1, 2026: its order status, dates and costs go stale from that day. Don't read it and don't type in it. Order and track from orders.ktubtu.com (§8); every job on one page is at orders.ktubtu.com/w.

7.1 Why it was retired

  • The sheet existed because JobTread's API couldn't take purchase orders. orders.ktubtu.com is now the record (every line, every purchase, who and when), and it writes order status, dates and purchased cost back to JobTread itself.
  • A cell typed in the sheet was the one way around signature before any order (§3): the order page and the workbook refuse an order on an unsigned job; a spreadsheet can't.
  • Costs and GP in the sheet are visible to anyone with the Drive link. orders.ktubtu.com shows them only to people with cost access.

7.2 Where each thing the sheet did lives now

  • A tab per job: the job's order sheet at orders.ktubtu.com (open the job).
  • Summary tab, every job at once, filter and sort: the workbook, orders.ktubtu.com/w. Every line is editable there by anyone with orders access, and costs and GP show only to people with cost access.
  • Change log tab: each line's history on the order sheet and in the workbook, by login and time.
  • The file itself stays in Drive (ktubloomfieldnj → .Project Management) for reference only. Its title says it is retired. If nobody misses it by mid-October 2026 it is deleted.

8. orders.ktubtu.com — ordering, purchases and profit

orders.ktubtu.com is the team's ordering front door. Sign in with your pricing.ktubtu.com login.

  • Home (/): every job with an order sheet, what's left to order, what's late.
  • Order sheet (open a job): set each line's order status, PO #, order date, ETA, received date, actual cost, who ordered it and how it was paid. It saves as you go. Under the expected cost, max $X is the buy sheet's spend ceiling: the most you can pay and still hold the 50% floor. It turns red when the actual cost goes over. (The old buy-sheet site, purchasing.ktubloomfield.com, now opens orders.)
  • Workbook (/w, or Workbook on any order sheet): the whole job as an editable sheet.
    • Change any number the JobTread sync provides (quantity, product, SKU, expected cost). Your number is kept on every later sync; the purple edge shows it, and ↺ JT puts JobTread's value back.
    • + Add a client: from JobTread, or by hand for work outside the automation. + Add line for anything not on the estimate.
    • Removing a line strikes it through; nothing is deleted. History shows who changed what, by login.
    • Signature before any order, here too. On an unsigned job a red banner says Do not order, and a line can't be marked ordered or given a PO #, order date or confirmation. A JobTread job unlocks when its final Selection Sheet is signed in JobTread (then ↻ Refresh from JobTread). A job added by hand or from ServiceMinder has no JobTread sheet: once the client has signed the paper Selection Sheet, tick Signed (paper) and say where the signed sheet is. Your name and the time are recorded, and it can be undone.

8.1 Purchases, invoices and profit

  • An actual cost on a line is the purchase: vendor, date purchased (order date) and amount. It goes straight into job costing, the same numbers as the intranet Job Costing tab and Monthly reporting. Enter a purchase once, on its line.
  • When the vendor's invoice reaches ktubtubilling@ and is matched to the job, the line links to it automatically if the vendor and the amount or PO match. The invoice amount then counts instead, so nothing is counted twice. You can also pick the invoice on the line.
  • Expected vs actual GP per job and across jobs is on the workbook for people with cost access.

8.2 ServiceMinder job costing

ServiceMinder has no way for other systems to write into a proposal's job costing (Margins). On a job's workbook, Post to ServiceMinder puts a note on the client's ServiceMinder contact listing each purchase (vendor · date purchased · actual amount) against the proposal number. Copy gives the same lines to paste into the proposal's Margins.

8.3 Who can see what

Set per person in pricing.ktubtu.com → Settings (the gear) → Team & access:

  • Orders: see and work orders.
  • Costs & profit: see and enter costs, actual amounts and GP.
  • Job costing: the intranet invoice queue and payment release.

Someone without Costs & profit sees orders with the money columns hidden and cannot enter an actual cost.

9.1 What orders writes back to JobTread

  • Per line, on the job's budget line (not on the signed Selection Sheet, which JobTread locks once approved): Status (✅Ordered, Received, ➡️Order Ready To Be Placed or N/A), Schedule: Date Ordered, Schedule: Date Received, Purchased Cost $.
  • Per job: Job Status, forward only and only through the ordering steps above. If the job is already further along, or someone set a status the ordering steps don't use, it is left alone.
  • When: a few seconds after you save on the order page or the workbook; anything missed goes on the 2-hourly refresh. Only changes are sent.
  • If you edit those fields in JobTread, your edit stays until the line changes again on orders — then orders' value is written. Keep ordering in orders so the two agree.
  • If it fails, the line's detail in the workbook says JobTread not updated and why; the schedule retries it every 6 hours.
  • JobTread can't take purchase orders through its API, so the PO # and vendor stay in orders.ktubtu.com.

Don't

  • Mark a selection Approved yourself — the client approves by signing.
  • Create a second job or a pile of draft "Selection" documents for the same client.
  • Put eight categories in one pick list on the Selections tab — the client will tick one.
  • Order from a rendering, a text message or an unsigned draft.

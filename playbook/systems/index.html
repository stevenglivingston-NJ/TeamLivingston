3. How our own tools are wired

You don't need this to do the work. It's here so nobody has to guess what breaks when something is down.

CodeGitHubEvery tool's code and every document on this site. Changes are reviewed, then published.
→
Runs onCloudflareHosts the intranet, pricing tool, Design Journey, lookbook and this playbook. Nothing runs on anyone's computer.
→
StoresSupabaseThe intranet's database and logins, the agents' briefs, the email queue, the ServiceMinder / JobTread syncs.
→
Tells peopleEmail & SlackSelection confirmations, order sheets, designer briefs and agent alerts go out through one queue, to team addresses only.
If something looks wrongCheck the intranet's Tech Health tab first: it shows which connection is down and which brief is stale. A board that shows yesterday's numbers usually means an agent didn't run, not that the data is wrong.

4. Where documents live

  • Standards and SOPs: this playbook. Edit the source, never a copy.
  • Job files: the JobTread job (signed documents, estimate, selections) and CompanyCam (photos).
  • Order tracking: the Job Tracker Google Sheet in Drive → .Project Management.
  • Business library: Drive folders 01 Company & HR to 07 Vendors & Products, KTU Resources. The intranet Library tab indexes them. Finance and HR folders are owner-only.
  • Email and text templates sent to clients: kept in the code, pasted into ServiceMinder. ServiceMinder holds copies, not the originals.
  • Scripts, training, vendor contacts: intranet Playbook, Vendors and Directory tabs.
